# First 14 days

Every day follows the voice protocol. Active phrases are a ceiling, not a quota: teach fewer when they cannot yet be used spontaneously.

## Day 0 — Baseline and sound map

**Mission:** introduce yourself for 90 seconds and survive a simple first-time conversation.

- Record an unscripted sample.
- Test numbers, time, names, country, residence, intentions and repair phrases.
- Map which Portuguese sounds disappear at natural speed.
- Create the first error ledger and list ten situations coming soon in Portugal.
- Preserve an honest zero point; do not teach to the baseline.

## Day 1 — Contact

**Mission:** greet, introduce yourself and ask three natural questions.

**Pattern:** `Eu sou...`, `Chamo-me...`, `Sou de...`, `Moro em...`, `E tu?` / `E o senhor?`

**Sound:** word stress and the contrast between clear and natural speech.

**Reality:** use one Portuguese greeting in a real interaction.

## Day 2 — The conversation safety net

**Mission:** keep the exchange alive when comprehension fails.

**Core phrases:** `Desculpe`, `Pode repetir?`, `Mais devagar, por favor`, `O que quer dizer...?`, `Percebi`, `Não percebi bem`, `Como se diz...?`

**Pattern:** polite questions with `pode` and `podia`.

**Test:** create three controlled misunderstandings that the learner must repair.

## Day 3 — Why Portugal?

**Mission:** explain the move to Portugal and ask follow-up questions.

**Pattern:** `Quero...`, `Vou...`, `Porque...`, `Gosto de...`, `Estou a aprender...`

**Sound:** reduced unstressed `e` and `o`; perception before production.

**Personal topic:** the move, climate, people, work and desired life.

## Day 4 — The café mission

**Mission:** order, modify the order, ask about something and pay.

**Pattern:** `Queria...`, `Para mim...`, `Tem...?`, `Pode trazer...?`, `A conta, por favor.`

**Sound:** `ão`, `m`/`n` as nasal markers and polite-request rhythm.

**Unexpected variation:** an item is unavailable or the order is wrong.

## Day 5 — Numbers, time and arrangements

**Mission:** make an arrangement without confusing the day or time.

**Pattern:** `A que horas?`, `Às...`, `Na segunda-feira`, `Antes/depois`, `Dá-me jeito`, `Não posso`.

**Test:** say five times at natural speed.

## Day 6 — Personal conversation

**Mission:** hold an eight-minute unscripted conversation about interests.

**Topics:** AI, product work, food, travel and entrepreneurship.

**Rule:** flow mode first; correction afterward.

**Human calibration:** if possible, have a native speaker judge the comprehensibility of 3–5 sentences.

## Day 7 — Week-one test

- new 90-second introduction;
- unseen café roleplay;
- three misunderstandings to repair;
- natural listening without text;
- all due phrases retrieved without a script;
- comparison with day 0 and only three priorities for week two.

## Day 8 — Daily routine

**Mission:** describe an ordinary day and ask about someone else's routine.

**Pattern:** present tense, `costumo`, `normalmente`, `de manhã`, `à tarde`, `à noite`.

**Sound:** connected words and weak function words.

## Day 9 — Food and preferences

**Mission:** explain likes, dislikes, tolerances and things to try.

**Pattern:** `Gosto de`, `Prefiro`, `Não como`, `Posso comer`, `É feito com...?`

**Personal topic:** Portuguese dishes and places to explore.

## Day 10 — Getting there

**Mission:** ask for and understand simple directions.

**Pattern:** `Onde fica...?`, `Como é que vou...?`, `em frente`, `ao lado`, `à esquerda/direita`.

**Listening:** draw or describe the route without seeing the text.

## Day 11 — Housing and area

**Mission:** describe housing needs and ask viewing questions.

**Pattern:** `Procuro`, `Preciso de`, `Há...?`, `Fica perto de...?`, `Quanto é...?`

**Unexpected variation:** noise, deposit or missing equipment.

## Day 12 — Product and personal projects

**Mission:** explain in simple Portuguese which problem a product solves.

**Pattern:** `Ajuda a...`, `Serve para...`, `O problema é...`, `A ideia é...`, `Estamos a desenvolver...`

**Rule:** clear meaning before technical vocabulary. Ask genuine follow-ups.

## Day 13 — Social invitation

**Mission:** invite, arrange details, accept and decline warmly.

**Pattern:** `Queres...?`, `Podemos...`, `Combinado`, `Talvez`, `Desta vez não posso`.

**Register:** distinguish informal and polite address.

## Day 14 — Integration test

**Scenario:** meet someone new, introduce yourself, explain the Portugal plan, arrange coffee, order and briefly discuss work. Add two misunderstandings and one practical problem.

**Pass condition:** complete the mission without a script, even if the Portuguese is not grammatically perfect.

**Afterward:** update the level hypothesis, speaking share, listening comprehension, active phrases and three error priorities. Generate week three from that evidence.
