# 12-week route

| Week | Operative mission                            | Language focus                                   | Personal application                |
| ---: | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
|    0 | Baseline and system setup                    | Sound map, comprehension, spontaneous speech     | Why Portugal and learning goals     |
|    1 | Establish contact and repair conversation    | Greetings, identity, `ser/estar`, politeness     | Introduce the learner and the move  |
|    2 | Food, cafés, numbers and arrangements        | Present tense, questions, articles, nasal sounds | Order, small talk and preferences   |
|    3 | Housing, neighbourhood and routines          | `há`, prepositions, reflexive verbs              | Viewings, rent and daily life       |
|    4 | Transport, directions and practical services | Imperative, time, movement verbs                 | Navigate and solve small problems   |
|    5 | Relationships and social life                | Modals, invitations, register                    | Connect, plan and decline naturally |
|    6 | Health, pharmacy and surprises               | Body, symptoms, needs, past                      | Explain a problem precisely         |
|    7 | Work, AI, product and personal projects      | Work vocabulary, explanations and cause          | Present a product and decisions     |
|    8 | Bureaucracy, bank, contracts and housing     | Formal register, documents, conditions           | Handle relocation administration    |
|    9 | Culture, news and opinions                   | Cohesion, nuance, agreement                      | Join a longer social conversation   |
|   10 | Stories, experiences and plans               | Past contrasts and future                        | Tell a personal story naturally     |
|   11 | Negotiation and complex situations           | Argument, hypotheses, clarification              | Meetings and misunderstandings      |
|   12 | Integration and Portuguese-first days        | Fluency, strategy, self-correction               | Final field test                    |

## Weekly rhythm

1. New mission, sound target and core phrases.
2. Pattern discovery and controlled dialogue.
3. Natural listening and an information gap.
4. Personal topic and longer conversation.
5. Unexpected version of the mission.
6. Real-world task or human conversation.
7. Unseen test, review and recalibration.

Formal recalibration happens after day 7, week 4 and week 8. Pause new content for a repair block when comprehension or mission completion stops improving.
